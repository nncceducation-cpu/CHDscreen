How the algorithm works

Fail: either reading below 90% at any screen.

Pass: at least one reading ≥95% AND difference ≤3 percentage points, with neither below 90%.

Borderline: all other combinations. Repeat in 1 hour. A third borderline screen is a failed screen.

This follows the supplied CHDscreen chart (page 5) and the linked CPS chart. A reading of 95% with 92% passes this chart. Local protocols may differ; confirm the approved protocol before clinical use.

Timing, limitations & references

Prefer 24–36 hours. If discharge is earlier, arrange screening and reliable follow-up; earlier screening is preferable to missing screening but produces more false positives. For NICU babies, infants born before 34 weeks, or babies receiving oxygen, use a clinician-directed pathway.

A passed screen does not exclude all congenital heart disease. Continue the newborn examination, including femoral pulses, and investigate clinical concerns regardless of the result.
